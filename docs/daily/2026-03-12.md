## What Happened

### Market Context
- Previous day (Mar 11) ended with a 56-pt sell-off from ~6820 to ~6749
- Evening session opened with continued bearish momentum: TREND_CONT_SHORT fired at 19:15 with ADX=26, MACD=-4.44, conf=0.903 — a very high-conviction short signal
- Price was at 6718 but had already dropped ~100 pts from the prior day's open (6820 → 6718)
- VX=24.9 — in the pullback penalty zone (VX 22–28), signal type `trend_cont` received +0.05 additive

### The Core Failure: Re-Entering a Spent Move Into Overnight Chop

All 4 trades were SHORT in what appeared to be a downtrend, but the market had already:
1. **Exhausted its primary sell leg** — 100+ pts had already been covered by the time Trade 1 fired
2. **Entered an overnight churn zone** — price oscillated 6706–6736 across all 4 trade windows
3. **Bounced against every entry** — each SL was tight (6.7–12pt) relative to the overnight noise range

The TREND_CONT_SHORT signal (Trades 1 & 2) requires 2 descending closes + ADX≥25 + MACD<0 — conditions that were met technically, but the **structural move was done**. The market was in a low-liquidity overnight chop, not an active trend leg.

### Why the Bot Kept Re-Entering

Each trade closed at SL → new signal fired → re-entered in same direction. This happened because:
- The EMA stack remained bearish (EMA9 < EMA21 < EMA50) throughout the churn
- ADX stayed 26–29 (above the 25 threshold for TREND_CONT, above 18 for EMA21_PB)
- CHOP guard did not activate — ADX was above the CHOP regime threshold
- No "consecutive loss" cooldown triggered until after Trade 4 (requires 5 consecutive)

## Root Cause Analysis

### Primary: Cascading Re-Entry After Exhausted Move
The strategy has no mechanism to detect that a trend leg is **spent**. Once price drops 80–100 pts in a session, new TREND_CONT/EMA21_PB signals should carry lower conviction — but the current system doesn't discount for "how far has the move already gone."

### Secondary: Consecutive SL Hits Without Cooldown Escalation
4 consecutive SL hits in 5 hours. The bot's consecutive-loss cooldown triggers at **5 losses** and imposes a 45-min pause — it never fired here. Even a 2-loss consecutive trigger with a 30-min cooldown would have broken the chain after Trade 2.

### Tertiary: Fix #13 Breakeven Trigger Too High for Overnight
At 3pts/$15, the breakeven trigger misses trades that peak at 1.5–2pt profit before reversing to full SL. For overnight (lower volatility, tighter price action), a **2pt/$10 threshold** may be more appropriate.
