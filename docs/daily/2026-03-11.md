## Fundamental Takeaway

These losses do **not** point to a broken risk gate or order execution defect. The recap's regime diagnosis is directionally correct — **overnight A/D pullbacks in choppy, elevated-VX conditions are the immediate problem** — but that is only the **proximate** cause.

The deeper issue is **trade expectancy math**:

> if the live system is taking trades around **~1:1 risk/reward** and winning only **37.5%** of them, the strategy has negative expected value regardless of regime filtering.

With the current running tally at **3W / 5L (37.5% WR)** and recent overnight pullbacks trading near **1.03:1 to 1.11:1 R:R**, the bot can still bleed even after removing some bad setups. Fewer low-edge trades would slow losses, but would not by itself repair the underlying edge.

### Where the current report and code agree

- **Trade 8 was borderline**: `ADX=25` is a low-conviction overnight pullback entry.
- **Overnight A/D signals** in choppy / high-VX structure are the correct area to investigate.
- **CHOP guard** and **RiskGate** appear to be functioning correctly.
- **Thin pre-London liquidity** is a real structural problem for pullback entries.

### What seems fundamentally wrong

There are **two layers** to the problem:

1. **Regime-fit weakness** — the bot is too willing to trust borderline overnight EMA21 pullbacks in mixed / choppy structure.
2. **Expectancy weakness** — the trades that are allowed through still have **too little payoff asymmetry** to overcome a sub-50% win rate.

Trade 7 is the clearest example. It is described as a success because it "correctly passed R:R validation" at roughly **1.03:1**, but that is only a repair of the previous worst-case bug. It is **not** strong math for an overnight pullback in a `VX=25.1`, downtrend / choppy environment.

### Where the current overnight fix is only partial

The overnight adjustment fixed the prior failure mode where TP compression made R:R outright poor. But the current setup still widens overnight risk more than reward:

- **SL** is widened via `ft_overnight_sl_mult: 1.2`
- **TP** is held flat via `ft_overnight_tp_mult: 1.0`

That means the system has stopped the worst asymmetry, but has **not** created a strong overnight edge. A trade that barely clears a `min_risk_reward_ratio: 1.0` gate is still structurally weak when signal quality and hit rate are unstable.

### Additional structural concern: no profit protection

Trade 7 was reportedly **+$25 unrealized** before reversing to a full stop. That highlights another gap:

- there appears to be **no meaningful breakeven / profit-lock behavior** protecting open gains on these overnight pullback trades

So the bot is not only taking near-1:1 entries, it may also be **giving back favorable excursion for free**.

### What does *not* seem wrong

- **RiskGate** appears to be doing its job — losses stayed small and bounded.
- **CHOP guard** likely helped, not hurt; the two blocked D signals were low-quality and probably correct to skip.
- The overnight TP fix **repaired the prior broken case**, but it should be viewed as a partial repair, not a complete solution.

### Most likely improvement areas

The next fixes should address both **quality** and **math**:

1. Require stronger ADX for overnight `A/D` pullbacks than during RTH.
2. Require clearer EMA21/EMA50 separation or slope overnight (not just alignment).
3. Raise the effective overnight reward threshold — a bare `min_risk_reward_ratio: 1.0` is too permissive for these setups.
4. Revisit overnight SL/TP asymmetry so widened overnight risk is matched by enough reward potential.
5. Add breakeven / profit-lock logic once a trade has achieved meaningful unrealized profit.
6. Be more skeptical of pullback entries during thin pre-London / early-Europe liquidity.
